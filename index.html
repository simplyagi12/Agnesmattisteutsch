About

One portrait only — the artwork remains the visual protagonist.

Agnes Mattis-Teutsch is a Romanian visual artist whose practice develops through colour, intuition, rhythm, layering and emotional memory. Her work moves between structure and spontaneity: grids, lines and repeated traces coexist with dense material surfaces and gestural fields.

Artist Statement

Working draft for the website.

Painting rarely begins for me with a fixed image. It begins with a state, a memory, a tension, or something that resists language.

I build and dismantle the surface through colour, transparency, gesture, repetition and accident. The grid appears as both structure and pressure; colour pushes against it, escapes it, returns to it. What remains is not a representation of an event, but its emotional residue.
